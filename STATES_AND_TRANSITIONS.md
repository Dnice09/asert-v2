### Defined States:

-   **Initial state**: `AWAITING_APPLICATION_FEES`
-   **Intermediate states**:
    -   `AWAITING_INSPECTION`,
    -   `AWAITING_BOARD_REVIEW`,
    -   `AWAITING_REGISTRATION_FEES`,
    -   `AWAITING_HFR_CODES`
-   **Failure states**:
    -   `APPLICATION_EXPIRED`,
    -   `FAILED_INSPECTION`,
    -   `FAILED_BOARD_REVIEW`,
    -   `REGISTRATION_EXPIRED`
-   **End state**:
    -   `FACILITY_REGISTERED`

### Defined Transitions:

-   **From** `AWAITING_APPLICATION_FEES` **to** `AWAITING_INSPECTION` **on** `PAY_APPLICATION_FEES`
-   **From** `AWAITING_APPLICATION_FEES` **to** `APPLICATION_EXPIRED` **on** `APPLICATION_EXPIRED`
-   **From** `AWAITING_INSPECTION` **to** `AWAITING_BOARD_REVIEW` **on** `PASS_INSPECTION`
-   **From** `AWAITING_INSPECTION` **to** `FAILED_INSPECTION` **on** `FAIL_INSPECTION`
-   **From** `AWAITING_BOARD_REVIEW` **to** `AWAITING_REGISTRATION_FEES` **on** `PASS_BOARD_REVIEW`
-   **From** `AWAITING_BOARD_REVIEW` **to** `FAILED_BOARD_REVIEW` **on** `FAIL_BOARD_REVIEW`
-   **From** `AWAITING_REGISTRATION_FEES` **to** `AWAITING_HFR_CODES` **on** `PAY_REGISTRATION_FEES`
-   **From** `AWAITING_REGISTRATION_FEES` **to** `REGISTRATION_EXPIRED` **on** `REGISTRATION_EXPIRED`
-   **From** `AWAITING_HFR_CODES` **to** `FACILITY_REGISTERED` **on** `GENERATE_HFR_CODE`

-   The `REVIEW_OUTCOME` event handles transitions based on guards.
